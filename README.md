# 🏠 VortexTech Data Science – Week 4 Report

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes 4,600 home sales in Washington State to identify key factors affecting property prices. The analysis provides actionable recommendations for a real estate company.

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. **Location** – Medina is the most expensive city ($2.05M average).
2. **Waterfront View** – Increases price by up to 238% in modern homes.
3. **Living Area** – Correlation of 0.54 with price.
4. **Building Year** – 1930s homes are the most expensive ($693K).
5. **Renovation** – Does NOT always guarantee higher value.
6. **Old vs Modern** – Old homes (1900-1930) are more expensive than modern (2000-2010) despite being smaller.
7. **Waterfront + Age** – Modern waterfront homes are the most expensive ($2.06M).

## 📈 Visualizations

## 💡 Recommendations
1. Target waterfront properties (highest ROI).
2. Invest in modern waterfront homes ($2.06M average).
3. Invest in historic homes (1900-1930) in prime locations.
4. Avoid modern homes without waterfront in average locations.
